About.

Artists submit a resume, updated portfolio, and project proposal by October 31, 2026. The proposal will outline the goals of the project, the area of Computer Science research in focus, the requested budget, and specific academic staff and/or equipment needed.

A panel consisting of representatives from the School of Computer Science and the Sydney College of the Arts at the University of Sydney will decide on two to five artists to sponsor for the duration of the program. Applicants will be assessed by project feasibility and relevance to Computer Science research.

Successful artists will be contacted and announced on December 1, 2026.
